💿 Did You Know
"Jeep" was written the night Kim was evacuated from LA during the wildfires. She and her collaborators packed up a session and wrote it in the desert. 🌵

The MV was filmed in Paris, France and the French countryside by director Leonie Miller-Aichholz. 🇫🇷

⚡ BREAKING
MAY 22, 2026 — T-7 DAYS
Kim Petras Is Finally, Actually, Truly Arriving
DETOUR — Her Debut Album — Drops May 29, 2026 via BunHead Records. Full Creative Control. No Sacrifices. This is everything.

For years, Kim Petras was pop's greatest open secret — the songwriter with credits on some of the decade's biggest records, the live performer who could hold any crowd, the personality that felt destined for legendary status. And yet, somehow, a proper debut album kept getting delayed, shelved, rescheduled, or taken away.

"My fans have waited long enough. I love u guys. I'm dropping Detour regardless."

Then came Polo. Then Freak It. Then I Like Ur Look. Then Need for Speed. Then Jeep — a country-inflected ballad co-written during the LA wildfires that somehow became her most critically praised single yet. And suddenly May 29, 2026 became the most circled date in every pop fan's calendar.

Inspired by Gwen Stefani's L.A.M.B., Fergie, The Pussycat Dolls, and M.I.A., Detour represents Kim Petras at maximum creative freedom. She fired a bunch of people. She cut off the label. She worked with trans musicians and collaborators who understood exactly what this album needed to sound like. And the result is, by all accounts, the album of 2026.
