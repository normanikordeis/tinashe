about detour

“The album is called Detour because it's like going off the rails and going off the road that I felt like I was being sent down.”

"It got to a point where I knew in my physical body that I just couldn't fake trying to be this perfect pop star anymore. I liked the idea of shapeshifting and playing with persona, but my body started rejecting it. I couldn't get out of bed in the morning, because I knew I had to pretend to be something I'm not."

"It's my pride and joy. I really shed, I think, a lot of my previous styles. I wanted to break all the rules and reinvent myself and work with exciting artists that I love, and be rebellious against traditional pop and how clean my vocals were."

"Detour is what I think the future of music sounds like, rather than how I fit into the current mainstream climate."

"Now that I'm releasing via BunHead Records, I get to release when I want. I know what my next album is. I know what the album after that is... I'll never put out anything that I don't think is great ever again. I'll have control."
